How this was made

This world was built live, as a cognitive-centaur experiment — a human and a large language model co-developing a story-world in conversation, with every decision captured in an Obsidian vault of interlinked markdown notes.

It began at Makers gonna Make, at The Melting Pot, Edinburgh — [date & lu.ma event link: TODO — paste details]. Rather than write the story first, we developed its underpinnings: the genre theory (a fantasy organised around a science-fictional novum), the economics and ecology of dragon-dung as an industrial fuel, the cast of perspectives, and the chains of consequence that follow from one accidental discovery.

The notes you can browse here are the actual working vault — not a tidied write-up. The graph shows how the ideas connect (each link is an Obsidian [[wikilink]]); the notes let you read them directly. The story itself (the other mode) is deliberately still placeholder: the experiment was about developing the ideas carefully before writing a word of prose.
