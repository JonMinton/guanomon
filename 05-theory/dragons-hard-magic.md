# Hard-magic dragons — literature survey & real-animal models

*Research scaffolding (2026-06-11) behind the [[dragons|dragon-society]] revision. Two
questions: (a) how have other writers given dragons a *rule-governed* biology or a
worked-out *sociology*, and what's transferable; (b) which **real** large, solitary,
long-lived predators (and long-distance communicators) supply principled models. Confidence
flagged inline. The project prizes the dragon's **mythic role as the ideal-type ultimate
predator above mechanistic explanation** — so the governing rule is: explain enough to make
the world legible (Sanderson), leave one numinous core un-explained (Le Guin/Tolkien).*

> **See also** [[genre-theory]] (the hard/soft-magic axis this sits on), [[dragons]] (the
> canon this informs), [[the-hum]] (the murmur), [[hummingbirds]].

## Part A — dragons given coherent biology or sociology in existing work

| Work | The system | Hardness | Transferable principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Novik, *Temeraire*** | dragons as a worked political/sociological *class* — different human institutions (British corps vs Chinese citizenship) produce different dragon polities | soft biology, **hard sociology** | *The same biology yields different cultures under different institutions* — ideal Rashomon fuel; **but** it works by making dragons **persons**, which is the myth-cost fork below |
| **McCaffrey, *Pern*** | bioengineered, telepathically bonded; **fire gated behind ingested fuel** (firestone); explicit reproductive rules; framed as SF, not fantasy | hard | *A dragon's signature power can be gated behind a consumed fuel* — near-exact precedent for **dung-as-fuel**; readers accept "dragon as chemical reactor" without losing grandeur |
| **Pratchett, swamp dragons** | digestive **combustion** chemistry — multiple stomachs distil flame-fuel from anything burnable; over-excitement → **they explode**; it's their main cause of death | hard (under comedy) | *Combustion as gut-chemistry with a fuel economy and a **failure mode*** — directly licenses our dragons as volatile reactors; borrow the failure-mode idea, keep the register grave |
| **Hobb, *Rain Wild*** | dragons hold **ancestral memory** as heritable knowledge; current generation **malformed/declining** from bad developmental conditions | medium | *Ancestral memory should be heritable **and fallible*** — degradable memory = legibility + a built-in decline/tragedy engine |
| **Le Guin, *Earthsea*** | dragons are oldest beings, *are* the Language of Making; dragon/human a primordial split (freedom vs knowledge) | deliberately **soft** | *Deep time + primordial memory by refusing mechanism* — the payoff of leaving the core un-explained |
| **Tolkien (Smaug) / Martin** | Smaug = intelligent, greedy, hypnotic, near-zero mechanism; Martin = anatomically plausible (wyvern body), **non-verbal beast, not person** | soft / medium | *Intelligence + greed + one uncanny power is enough*; *keeping the dragon **non-verbal** keeps it a wild apex predator* (Martin) |
| **Shannon, *Priory*** | Eastern dragons immortal, tied to a ~500-yr **comet cycle**, dormant ("Long Slumber") between peaks | medium | *Tie longevity to a long astronomical/geological **cycle** with dormancy* — mechanises deep time and "dispersed, then congregating" |
| **D&D *Draconomicon* / *Monster Hunter*** | D&D: explicit **age categories** scale size/power/worldview; MH Elder Dragons: defy taxonomy, act as **ecological regulators** | hard (rules) | *Make age a stat that scales power & worldview*; *let the apex be an ecological **force**, not a member of a food web* |

**The central fork (load-bearing for us):** Novik's rich dragon *sociology* and the
Smaug/Martin *ideal-type predator myth* pull opposite ways — sociology tends to make dragons
**persons**. The escape the project takes (and that the real biology below licenses): **route
all dragon society through the [[the-hum|murmur]]** — a non-verbal, infrastructural,
clan-marked, ground-borne channel. That buys genuine sociology (dispersed clans, dialect,
coordinated congregation, transmitted memory) on a *hard* substrate **while the dragon never
speaks and never stops being the mythic apex predator.** Adopted.

## Part B — real-world models (the rigorous backbone)

### §territory — solitude is arithmetic
- **Carbone & Gittleman (2002):** ~10,000 kg of prey supports only ~90 kg of a given
  carnivore *regardless of carnivore size*; carnivore density scales to mass^−0.75. Bigger
  predators **must spread out**. *[Established.]*
- Home range rises with body mass and **steepens with how carnivorous the diet is and how
  unproductive the habitat** — obligate meat-eaters need disproportionate range. *[Established,
  with scatter.]* Tigers, leopards, polar bears, saltwater crocs, Komodo, great whites: **solitude is the default**, driven by competition for a thin, expensive prey base.
- **Design:** solitude isn't temperament, it's the arithmetic of being that large and that
  high in the web — so dragons are *necessarily* rare, dispersed, and dependent on
  **long-distance** signalling. *Very hard; zero myth-cost — it makes the dragon grander and
  lonelier.* **Adopted** ([[dragons]] §1).

### §sound — the murmur is physically defensible ⭐
- **Elephant seismic communication:** rumbles ~14–35 Hz couple into the ground and travel as
  **Rayleigh (surface) waves**; ground transmits low frequency with **~3 dB loss per
  distance-doubling vs ~6 dB in air** — *ground beats air at range.* Reception via **cushioned,
  sensor-rich feet** (Pacinian corpuscles, bone conduction) and a **freeze-and-press**
  localising posture (O'Connell-Rodwell). *[Established.]*
- **Whales:** blue/fin song ~14 Hz ducted by the ocean **SOFAR channel** — detected ~965 km,
  theoretically channelable >6,000 km. **Sperm-whale codas** define **vocal clans** of
  thousands across ocean basins; calves *learn* them. *[Established.]*
- Alligators' near-infrasound "water dance"; frogs/golden moles using substrate waves —
  texture. *[Established.]*
- **Design (core of the project):** pin the **air-song** to low frequency (whale/elephant
  airborne component) and the **murmur** to **~10–40 Hz Rayleigh ground waves**. You get for
  free: (1) a real reason ground-song beats air at distance; (2) a **reception organ** —
  dragons "read" the rock through belly/feet, with a still, pressing posture; (3) a
  **substrate-dependent** comms map (carries through bedrock, dies in soft sediment/swamp;
  possibly a geological low-velocity layer ducts it like SOFAR — explains the un-wired deep
  carrying it continent-wide); (4) humans feel infrasound as **dread/pressure/nausea, not
  sound** — perfect for a Rashomon "everyone experiences it differently" effect; (5)
  **clan/lineage identity encoded in dialect** without speech — sociology without
  personhood. *Very hard; deepens dread.* **Adopted** ([[dragons]] §§3,6 + mechanics ruling).
- **Covertness from the physics (per Jon, 2026-06-11):** a denser, stiffer medium carries
  low frequency **further per unit energy** (the ~3 dB vs ~6 dB / distance-doubling point) —
  so the same reach costs **less power** in rock than in air. The dragons can therefore
  **whisper**: murmur at intensities well below the human detection floor, and **fold the
  timing into ambient industrial vibration** so even the residue reads as machine-tremor.
  Covertness is thus *built into the medium*, not bolted on. **Adopted** ([[dragons]]
  mechanics ruling).

### §longevity — deep time, with one clash to manage
- **Greenland shark ~270–512 yrs** (maturity ~150), bowhead ~211, ocean quahog ~507 —
  longevity coupled to **slow metabolism, slow growth, very late maturity**. *[Established.]*
- **Design:** dragon longevity should imply **slow maturation, rare reproduction** (juveniles
  precious → reinforces low density) and an old dragon as a **living archive** of cycles —
  the hard route to "long/cyclic view of history," no magic needed. *Hard; deepens myth.*
- ⚠ **Clash flag:** real extreme longevity is **cold-driven**, which fights a fire-associated
  dragon. **Reconciliation (adopted):** the dung-reactor metabolism runs **slow, hot and
  extraordinarily efficient** — longevity from efficiency, not cold. Leave it at that; don't
  lecture.

### §memory — "ancestral memory," the hard version and the soft fork
- **Elephant matriarchs** hold decades of route/water knowledge; older leaders measurably
  raise group survival (drought memory across 35–45 yrs; correct threat-ranking of lions).
  **Humpback song** is socially learned and undergoes basin-wide **"song revolutions"**
  spreading west→east over ~2 yrs. *[Established.]*
- **Design — the fork:** *hard version* = long life + a learned, transmitted, dialect-bearing
  **song-culture** that functions like ancestral memory (route, kill, territory, past
  congregations, and the **memory of prior civilisational cycles**). *Soft version* = literal
  inherited/genetic memory (Hobb/Le Guin), kept mysterious. **Project choice: mostly hard
  (transmitted song-culture) with a soft seam left open** (perhaps the blood remembers too) —
  best of both, and the cyclic-history portent rides either. **Adopted** ([[dragons]] §4).

### §aggregation — mostly solitary, periodically congregate
- **Komodo** solitary but gather at a kill and to **mate-wrestle** (seasonal); **brown bears**
  at salmon runs. Rule: a **concentrated super-resource or a mating/ritual season** overrides
  territoriality. *[Established.]*
- **Design:** dragons' rare rites (mating, the putting-down of upstarts, territory
  negotiation) need a trigger that beats solitude. Because solitude is the default, **the
  congregation is automatically momentous** — a natural set-piece and Rashomon convergence;
  the murmur is *how* a dispersed population times it across distance. **Adopted** ([[dragons]]
  §2).

## What this changes in canon (summary)
- **Reversed:** the earlier "the keeping *created* dragon society / wild dragons were
  asocial." Now: dragons **always** had a dispersed society + air-song; domestication
  **concentrated, threatened, and drove it underground** (the murmur). Determinism *sharpens*
  ([[dragons]] §society closing note).
- **Adopted:** solitude-as-arithmetic; air-song humans misread; the murmur as a
  physically-grounded ground-wave transposition hidden in industrial vibration; literacy/the
  written-language gift; ancestral memory (hard, with a soft seam); the **cyclic-history /
  wait-it-out** portent; the balance-of-power instinct **inherited and turned inward** (the
  wing-breaking).
- **Held un-explained on purpose** (the numinous core): what the oldest memory actually
  *contains*, and what — if anything — the dragons are *for*. Don't answer these.
