# Korrin — the dwarf fuel-engineer *(provisional name)*

> **Provisional name** (rev. 2026-05-31) — flag for confirmation. A **non-POV** character,
> seen inside [[brask|Brask's]] chapter.

The younger dwarf **fuel-engineer** who co-designs the early **Hummingbirds** with
[[brask|Brask]]: Brask brings the fork-guidance (the [[the-hum|Hum]] turned from carrying a voice
to *steering* a thing), Korrin brings the controlled guano (the dial between slow-burn and blast).
The two novums fused into a guided munition by two men who each command one half.

**Disposition — Brask's twin.** A **narrow meritocrat** who cares only whether the work is good
and is genuinely baffled that anyone minds species or guild. He is the one person Brask registers,
because competence is the only social category Brask can see; the two "had never understood why a
person was supposed to care about that." Where [[grundrak|Grundrak]] is *acutely* class-conscious,
Korrin (like Brask) is post-tribal by temperament, not virtue.

**Kept separate from [[grundrak|Grundrak's]] line** — deliberately *not* Grundrak's son / the
[[the-princess|Princess's]] husband, to avoid over-neatness (decided 2026-05-31).

**Sappers' (fuel) + Hummers' (signal)** programme, no names. [[guilds]]
