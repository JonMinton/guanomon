**Account genre:** clinical case-book — extracts from a professional log spanning ~13 years.
The laundering register *sincerely inhabited*: where the [[the-guild|Guild]] launders for an
audience, the Physician launders for himself, in real time, on the page.
**Register:** low/plain family (with [[grundrak|Grundrak]], [[brask|Brask]], [[marga|Marga]])
but a distinct flavour: hedged, formulaic, passive-clinical, quantity-precise. The feeling is
*managed*, not absent — discipline, with leaks. His signature device is the **margin/record
distinction**: what he half-knows goes into margins, then gets formally disowned ("The margin
is not the record").

## Role in the system
**Physician-in-Ordinary to the Source** — and the office is the joke the chapter never makes:
it was the *crown's* office. Taster to the royal body, keeper of its waters, an inheritance
of court medicine going back generations. The body that matters moved, and the office moved
with it, *downward* (the [[above-and-below|above→below inversion]] written into one man's
job). His grandmother tasted for a queen; he tastes for the Dragon. The art is unchanged.
The patient is larger.

He is unnamed — like the [[the-king|King]], he *is* an office; unlike the King, he never
noticed the office change masters. (His case-book is "the property of the Guild": even his
record is owned.)

  Same event as the Dragon's account, opposite laundering — the Rashomon method applied to

- **The in-chamber Hummingbird trial** ([[hummingbirds]] breadcrumb): stimulus trial, response

## The dual mandate (per Jon, 2026-06-10 — load-bearing)
His office holds **two conflicting duties**: the **care of the patient** (pushes against
overfeeding) and the **steadiness of the yield** (pushes toward it). **He is guided by the
former, and mindful he serves only at the pleasure of the Guilds** — his warrant names both
duties, "no provision is made for any case in which the two part company," and it is renewed
yearly. Every overridden recommendation in the case-book is the two duties parting company
while he keeps the post. His compromise is never corrupt advice — the recommendations stay
honest — it is *compliance with the verdict*: he records, files, renews, and does not pursue.
The committee exists so that the choice between his duties is never formally his; the
chapter's quiet horror is how grateful he is for that.

## What he sees
Everything, numerically. He is the best-informed human in the book about the thing the book
is about — the only one holding the aggregate data of the herd's decline.

## What he's structurally blind to (or won't look at)
He is the human mirror of the **contented suppressor dragons** ([[dragons]] §suppression):
every incentive points him to the humane reading, and he takes it, *knowing and not knowing
in the one mind at the one time*. The doublethink the Dragon describes in her own kind,
demonstrated in a human, in his own hand. His tragedy-shape: he keeps perfect records of a
catastrophe and files them where records are filed. The one thing he never does is *pursue
the thought* — and he says so.
