## The decisions (ranked by consequence)

### 1. Adopt the two new accounts? — *lean: YES, as one package with the ch. 14 diet*
The room's verdict: **ADOPT both** (structural editor formally; warden passes; the blind
reader ranked the Physician 4th most distinct voice in the book). But adoption is
**package-conditional**: the Physician without cutting the Dragon's chapter double-bills
the reader; the cut without the Physician re-opens the GPT review §2 problem. One decision.
- Cast becomes 14 chapters / 12 narrating voices; the room rules the **document quota
  full** (any future transcript/ledger/filed-form chapter is presumptively rejected) and
  the female-instrument-body rhyme **deliberately extended** to a fourth term (log in
  [[decisions]] if adopted).
- If rejected: drop the two chapter files and revert the renumbering; the world notes
  (dragon society, hummingbirds, keeping-era) can survive either way.

### 2. The chapter-14 diet (existing canon — untouched, awaiting your call)
Target ~3,364 → ~2,600 words. The structural editor's cut list (full detail in
[[writers-room-2026-06-10]] and the agent report): compress the sweetness/unwell paragraph
~40%; **cut** the grating/portion-control paragraph (now ch. 11's, near-verbatim) salvaging
"I understand them… the single thing they have never once thought to measure"; halve the
stimulus-trial restaging (keep "He watched my face… I gave him nothing" + the arithmetic
turn); cut the keeper-procedural beats of the wing-breaking and the rest-caves paragraph
(keep "the two ends of one refusal" and the whole "Your own will do it. Gently. And mean
it." movement — irreplaceable); trim the requisition list (keep the hoard-return mechanism
entire — the Marga payoff); trim the murmur tutorial by a third (keep broadcast-vs-murmur,
the flock simile, "you cannot break the wings of a pattern").
**Sub-decision (split room):** end on *"They think it is docility."* and cut "Soon, now. /
Soon."? The blind reader wants it gone ("a thriller sting on a literary book"); lean:
**cut it** — patience is the chapter's whole argument and the double "Soon" hurries it.

### 3. The dossier frame — *lean: ADOPT, minimum dose*
One-line provenance epigraphs per chapter, bureaucratically dry (dull-naming register,
never clever), dated in **the year of the keeping** — solving the GPT review's temporal
anchors diegetically and unifying the half-promised collection gestures already on the
page (Marga's asker, Brask's "meant to be a record", the two new filed documents).
**Design rule: exactly two chapters carry nothing — 10 (Dalla-private) and 14 (the
Dragon)** — the unit and the source, the two accounts the apparatus never filed. The
kingdom's complete self-record, with an absence shaped like its foundation. **Hard guard:
never mark the gaps.** (Knock-on: Marga's one-off square-bracket aside — the blind
reader's first confusion — should be resolved in the same pass; either cut the brackets or
make them a convention.)

### 4. The materialist one-liners (existing canon, high leverage, cheap)
1. **Guild address**: add the revenue clause — "and the fleets return laden" / "the
   granaries of the protectorates now provision the husbandry of the source" — closing the
   grain→livestock→lairs loop Liesse's fleets imply (and fixing the report-logic: it
   currently brags costs, never revenue).
2. **Canonise the Sappers' Guild as dwarf-officered, human-manned** (one sentence in
   [[labour-and-order]]) — resolves the "our own people on the drying-floors" wobble *and*
   the dwarven-labour class gap; optional half-line of Marga texture ("the dwarven
   pipe-crews who knock off at a bell we never hear").
3. **Garran, one paragraph**: the Peacekeeper beat canon promises ([[guilds]]: "subplot
   inside Garran") and the page lacks — the men he trained who took the home posting, in
   his register of shame.
4. **Liesse, one further struck passage** (optional): what became of her country's lair —
   ties colonial policy to herd-expansion ("the wild-recruited generation") and the
   Censor would strike exactly that.
5. Decide-and-file (no page time): committee composition; a Physicians' College; the
   absent church; the Compact as guild instrument under the royal seal (the seal/coin
   rhyme available: the powerless King's face on the wax that presses Liesse).

### 5. The voice pass (existing canon — the biggest craft job on the menu)
Per the room's strongest convergence (the **aphorism engine**): a sentence-level pass on
chs. 02, 03, 05, 06, 08, 10, 14 to break the shared epigram machinery. Specific targets
from the line editor and blind reader (quotes in [[writers-room-2026-06-10]]): make the
King's epigram shape exclusively his; rehouse his "who can reach whom" line (Garran and
the Dragon keep the rhyme); de-aphorise Garran/Princess/Marga's closers; fix the identical
ironic-denial endings of chs. 08 and 10; settle the cage-epigram on the Dragon alone;
coarsen Marga's "snow that's learned to gleam." [[voice-differentiation]] needs its
budgets renegotiated against the page (Grundrak and the King currently exceed them ~2–5×),
Vesser's number-rule inverted (wound-numbers allowed, neutral quantities forbidden), and a
**register of licensed rhymes** added (the Princess/King coin; Garran/Dragon reach;
Liesse/Princess warmth-and-doors counterpoint).
