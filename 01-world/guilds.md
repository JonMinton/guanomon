# Guilds — the concepts, and the characters who carry them

> **Principle (load-bearing — keep returning to it):** a **guild is a concept / institution,
> never a character.** Each guild's *human* story must reach the reader through an
> **individual representative.** "The Guild" may supply an institutional *voice* (an aired
> bulletin, a charter), but the lived experience of every guild is carried by a person. This
> note maps guild → representative and flags the gaps so no guild is left as a faceless
> abstraction.

## The gaps, ranked (recommendations)
1. **A collection-crew labourer — the missing proletarian POV. ✓ DONE (2026-05-30):**
   **[[marga]]**, the shoveller from Scene 1, developed into a full account — the
   working-class voice the class themes demanded *and* the human face of the otherwise-
   faceless [[the-guild|Guano Guild]]. Pays off the cold open; rhymes with [[dalla]] (both
   went into the dung; one fell and became famous, one kept shovelling). Her genre is *the
   un-genre* — plain realism as the truth-check on everyone else's self-dramatisation.
2. **A sapper — DECIDED: not now (folded).** *(2026-05-30.)* A dedicated sapper POV
   (explosives handling — the coup, [[guano|repotentiation]], the [[story-bible|Hummingbirds]],
   the new lethal work) was the strong-optional gap; left **unfilled** to hold cast size.
   [[grundrak]] carries the applied-guano-violence angle for now; revisit only if a chapter
   genuinely needs a sapper from the inside.
3. **Peacekeeper — fold into [[garran|Garran]]** (already a subplot) unless it earns its own face.
4. **Beekeeper — keep as texture** in [[labour-and-order]], not a POV.

> **Cast-size caution:** ~10 accounts / 8 narrators already ([[plot-beats]]). The **labourer**
> is the one I'd call essential; the **sapper** a strong optional; the rest fold. Add
> deliberately — every new account must do character *and* structural work, or it's the
> "inert glue" failure mode.
