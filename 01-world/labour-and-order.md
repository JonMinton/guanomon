# Labour and order — the reorganised economy and the monopoly of violence

> A world-entity note. The novum doesn't only change *who rules* — it reorganises **work**
> and **violence** across the whole society: new sectors, new hazards, new enforcers; an
> overall *fall* in deaths bought with new kinds of death and new kinds of coercion. The
> political economy of the transformation — and prime [[the-guild|Guild]]-laundering material.

## New work (the labour market the novum makes)
- **Dwarven logistics & repotentiation — control below, *risk above*.** The dwarves own the
  guano supply chain's *physical* layer: they pipe **slurry** — wet, inert [[guano|muck]] —
  safely through **underground pipes** (consistent with the [[guano|wet/dried gate]]: *move the
  inert form, arm it on site*). But the **dangerous** step — **repotentiation** (drying and
  **compression** back into explosive, substantial injury risk) — is done at **compressors
  sited above ground**, on human labour. The dwarves **own and direct** the whole network
  without **bearing** its hazard: pipes below (safe, dwarven), compressors above (dangerous,
  human). The siting is a **choice, not a constraint** — it offloads the risk upward (the
  [[above-and-below|risk inversion]]) behind a respectable safety rationale the [[the-guild|Guild]]
  launders ("explosive work kept clear of the tunnels" — *whose* tunnels?).
- **Above ground — apiaries.** The [[guano|honey catalyst]] needs bees, so **beekeeping**
  becomes a major new sector: new agricultural work, new guild(s), new dependence. The honey
  that arms the fuel is farmed in the open air while the fuel itself moves in the dark.
  - The bees are **footbees** — so named because the adults run about **a foot long.** The
    name is flatly descriptive (the [[the-hum|dull-naming]] principle — world-remaking things
    named like livestock) yet it *also* names the danger: a foot-long bee is a genuinely lethal
    animal, which is why apiary work kills (below) and why a "sting" is now something people die
    of. The banality of the monstrous: they named the foot-long killer after its length and got
    on with the harvest.

## New hazards (the death the progress narrative doesn't count)
The **footbees** are a **violent, lethal kind** (and a foot long), so apiary work kills and
maims at rates the old agrarian economy never did. The **above-ground compressor stations**
(drying and compressing an explosive — the danger the dwarves [[above-and-below|pushed up off
themselves]]) are their own new category of lethal accident. **Workplace death rises** — and
it rises *on the surface*, by design.

> **The human instance:** [[marga|Marga's]] husband died of a **sting** (an apiary bee) —
> mentioned flatly and *unexplained* in her chapter, so it reads as ordinary grief until the
> reader has this economy and decodes it. He is the specific, uncounted death behind the
> [[the-guild|Guild's]] "mortality down" headline. See [[marga]] (handle with restraint).

> **The Hobbesian trade-off (the dark heart of this note).** Yet *net* mortality **falls** —
> because the same overwhelming, concentrated power that ended contested war (the
> [[the-king|coup]] settled who rules; [[story-bible|guano firepower]] makes rebellion
> futile) also ends the chronic civil conflict that used to kill far more. A **monopoly of
> violence** — a Leviathan — trades the war of all against all for an *enforced* peace.
> Deaths aren't so much *reduced* as **redistributed and recategorised**: from visible,
> heroic war-death to mundane, invisible workplace-death. *(In-world the [[the-guild|Guild]]
> counts the wars-not-fought and never counts the apiary dead — perfect annual-report
> laundering. Boxer guard: carry it by the statistic and the funeral, never by a lecture on
> Hobbes.)*

## New enforcers — the Peacekeepers (a Slayers' Guild spin-off)
The upheaval breeds **dissent** — displaced trades, dangerous new work, a social order
inverted — and the [[garran|Slayers'/Warriors' Guild]], already hunting for a role, **spins off
a Peacekeepers' Guild** to suppress it. They are the Leviathan's **domestic fist**: at times
quite violent in putting down unrest. The bitter circuitry: **the displaced policing the
displaced** — warriors made obsolete by the change now beating the people aggrieved by the
same change. Hard power turned *inward* (rhyming with the [[the-king|coup's]] hard power
turned on sovereignty — see [[plot-beats|the taxonomy of submission]]).

> **Two faces of one concentration of power:** a **fist** (guano firepower, the
> [[story-bible|Hummingbirds]], the Peacekeepers) and a **voice** (the [[the-hum|Hum]],
> [[dalla|Dalla's]] celebrity, the Guild's myth). The Leviathan has both, and the story's
> hard/soft-power split runs right through it.
