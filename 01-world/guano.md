# Guano — the First Novum

> A world-entity note. **Guano is the First Novum** — the single rule-governed
> innovation the whole world reorganises around (see [[genre-theory]] for what "novum"
> means). This note is its conceptual home; the energy-economy **mechanics and timeline**
> remain the single source of truth in [[story-bible]].

## What it is
Dragon dung, discovered to be an **extraordinarily potent fuel** (and fertiliser). The
discovery inverts the world's relationship to dragons: not slaying but **husbandry** —
you farm the dragon and collect the output. Hoarded treasure becomes irrelevant; the
metabolic product outvalues the gold the dragon sleeps on. Dragon-*slayers* become
villains (they destroy critical energy infrastructure — see [[dern]]).

## Why it is the engine of everything
Guano is not just *a* discovery; it is the **cause from which the rest of the world
follows.** Two consequences are load-bearing enough to have their own notes:

1. **It gives rise to the Second Novum.** The mages, displaced by guano, try and fail to
   reproduce its *power* — and in failing invent **mass communication** instead. Guano
   doesn't just out-compete magic; it *redirects* magic into [[the-hum|the Hum]].
2. **It inverts the world's power axis from above to below.** Value now comes from
   *underground* — the dragon's deep chamber, dwarven extraction and processing — so
   power drains from the surface (human, royal, old) to the subterranean (dwarven, new).
   See [[above-and-below]].

> **One cause, two revolutions.** The First Novum produced both the Second Novum (the
> Hum) and the above→below inversion. Everything else in the world — the coup, the
> [[story-bible|tunnels you bow in]], [[dalla|Dalla's]] coerced celebrity, the
> [[story-bible|Hummingbirds]] — descends from that single discovery.

## Hard novum (maximally so)
Guano is the *hardest* possible novum — it isn't magic at all, it's **chemistry/biology**,
fully legible and economically meaningful. It satisfies *both* Sanderson's "legible
rules" and Suvin's "cognition as science." It is the hard pole against which
[[the-hum|the Hum's]] softness is measured. See [[genre-theory]] §5 (the world's two
novums).

## The resource base is *alive* (the hidden fragility)
Guano looks like a stock to be mined, but its source is a **population of living,
intelligent agents** ([[dragons]]). That changes the scarcity from *depletion* (slow,
foreseeable) to *dependency* (sudden, mispriced). The herd is degrading under the feeding
regime (the [[the-dragon|Dragon's]] diabetes is the first symptom, not a one-off), and the
civilisation's instinct — recruit *more* dragons to cover the shortfall — increases its
exposure while feeling like growth. They think they're miners; they're zookeepers who
forgot the animals are smarter than them. **The economy is more fragile than it knows.**
Full treatment + the determinism-safe "collapse/uprising" options: [[dragons]].

## The discovery problem
Why it wasn't found centuries earlier is a known weak point, resolved by **lampshade**:
nobody had ever been soiled this way before because the discipline was always *toward the
treasure, away from the dragon's filth*. [[dalla|Dalla]] voices it. See
[[discovery-gating]].
